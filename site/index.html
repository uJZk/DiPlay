Your iPhone.
At home in your car.

Maps, music and conversations on your car display. TiPlay brings wired and wireless CarPlay to compatible Android head units, with the familiar DiAuto interface.

Download APK ↓How to install

No jailbreak, dongle, account or authentication server required.

Install on the car, not the iPhone. Android 9+; Wi-Fi Direct is now available on Android 9 where the firmware supports it. Wireless uses the built-in car hotspot, Wi-Fi Direct or Existing Wi-Fi / Same LAN. Compatibility depends on the head unit, firmware and iOS version.

These projects focus on BYD cars. They may work on other brands, but other brands are unsupported and there are no plans to add support or fix brand-specific incompatibilities.

A familiar drive

01

Install on your car

  1. While parked, download the APK and install it using the method supported by your head unit.
  2. Open TiPlay and allow the requested Bluetooth, Wi-Fi/location and microphone permissions.
  3. Turn on the car’s built-in hotspot (5 GHz if available). In Settings → Connection setup, select Built-in car hotspot and save its exact name and password. Keep phone Wi-Fi and Bluetooth on, pair with the car, then tap Connect phone and accept the phone prompts.
  4. For wired use, connect a USB data cable and choose Connect with USB. Approve any USB, Trust or local VPN prompts.

ADB is not required for everyday use. APK installation options depend on the car. No manual MAC address entry.

New in 0.2.13

  • Wi-Fi Direct on compatible Android 9 firmware, with station-aware Auto channel choices. A legacy requested channel is not a confirmed operating channel.
  • Better wireless discovery: advertise the usable access-point IPv4 address, handle unavailable Android Bonjour/NSD services and preserve already-rendering handoff sessions.
  • Safer USB transport switching, permission verification and USBMUX replies with validated four-byte trailers; wireless startup also tolerates an unavailable USB system service.
  • Live dock and split-screen view areas, an experimental side panel and optional square-canvas rotation without reconnecting. Square canvases are checked against the selected decoder; live picture controls also support compatible cluster video.
  • Finer dashboard-map details with the 125% option on capable decoders, a 300 ms waiting/map cross-fade and day/night waiting screens. Turn cards survive brief wireless replacement within their existing expiry window.
  • DiLink 3 cluster projection setup corrections, optional DiLink 4 routing/calibration and recovery. Existing verified firmware gates for HUD output remain in place.
  • Remove TCP batching delay from touch commands, restore the enabled wheel-key service safely, and correct microphone timing for the observed 24 kHz Opus profile; this is not a general Siri fix.
  • Car-button image customization from Settings, clearer home/settings layouts, battery-protocol fallback and Traditional Chinese (Taiwan), bringing the app to seven languages.
  • Buffered Apple Music/main music audio and experimental DiLink 3 call controls are optional and off by default. Dashboard caller/timer output has a separate default-off setting and needs ADB; vehicle/audio acceptance needs release testing.
  • Hotspot repair on supported Android 13+ firmware requires explicit Check/Apply/Restore actions; it never runs automatically. Android 9+ remains the minimum; firmware limitations, connection failures and stutter can remain. Higher resolution can increase decoder load. Test experimental vehicle options while parked.
Release notes & checksums ↗

Before you try it

A public preview, not an Apple-certified product. Authentication uses an experimental accessory identity recovered from public firmware; future acceptance is not guaranteed. Some head units still show stuttering or ignore icon-size changes. Start with 30 fps and HEVC off; try a lower resolution if needed.

Updates & signing

TiPlay uses its own app package, com.ujzk.tiplay. It installs alongside DiPlay and does not keep DiPlay’s settings. Install later TiPlay releases over the previous one to keep settings. Test builds use a separate app package.

Please send fresh reports from 0.2.13

1. Update to 0.2.13, reproduce the issue and note the failure time. 2. Open Settings → Diagnostics → Save diagnostic report. Android 10+ normally saves to Downloads/TiPlay; Android 9 uses a file picker. If storage or the picker is unavailable, follow the fallback confirmation and use View/Share. 3. Review the TXT and remove passwords and private details. Reports are not uploaded automatically. 4. Attach it to your existing issue: https://github.com/uJZk/DiPlay/issues Or create a new one: https://github.com/uJZk/DiPlay/issues/new/choose Include vehicle/head-unit model, DiLink/Android/firmware, iPhone/iOS, connection type, authentication backend (Local or USB/CH341), relevant settings, reproduction steps, expected/actual result and failure time.
